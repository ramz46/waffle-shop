Uncompromising Quality & Provenance

Why Every Bite Feels Like Pure Celebration

🍫

Imported Callebaut Chocolate

We never use synthetic syrups. Our melted fountains run with 54.5% Belgian dark chocolate, silky milk cocoa, and velvety white vanilla ganache.

🌾

24-Hour Fermented Brioche Batter

Our Liege waffles are crafted from slow-fermented yeast dough folded with coarse pearl sugar that caramelizes to a crunchy glass glaze in heavy cast irons.

🫐

Farm-Direct Seasonal Fruit

Hand-selected strawberries, blackberries, figs, and Meyer lemons picked from local organic orchards and reduced into in-house compotes.
