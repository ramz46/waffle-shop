The Mobile Kitchen Theater

Authentic French & Belgian Event Hardware

No chafing warmers or pre-cooked rubbery crepes. We bring authentic commercial culinary equipment straight to your venue.

🥞

Krampouz Cast-Iron Billigs

Heavy machined cast-iron griddles forged in Brittany, France operating at 210°C. Delivers 60-second paper-thin sweet crepes and lace-edged savory buckwheat galettes.

Heat Level: 210°C / 410°F
🧇

35-Pound Belgian Liege Irons

Deep 4x7 grid presses radiating intense heat that melts pure Belgian pearl sugar without scorching the cold-fermented brioche dough interior.

Iron Mass: 35 lbs Seasoned Iron
☕

Dual-Boiler Mobile Espresso

Commercial Italian espresso cart featuring dual wands, rotary pumps, and organic dairy & oat milk micro-foaming for café-level cortados and iced shakeratos.

Pressure: 9 Bar Rotary Pressure
🍫

Callebaut Molten Wells

Thermostatically stabilized fondue wells maintaining pure Belgian 70% dark chocolate and dulce de leche at a silky 32°C for cascading drizzling.

Tempering Point: 32°C Silk Cascade

Catering Details & Policies

We typically require an 8ft x 6ft service footprint for our dual crepe and waffle cart. We carry both high-output electrical units (standard 110V/20A) and whisper-quiet battery inverter power for al fresco vineyard settings without dedicated outlets.
All Brittany galettes are made from 100% stone-ground certified buckwheat flour (sarrasin), which is naturally gluten-free. For severe celiac guests, we operate a dedicated, sanitized cast-iron griddle with separate utensils to eliminate any cross-contamination.
