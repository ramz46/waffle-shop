Handcrafted in San Francisco Since 2018

Honoring Old-World Tradition with Modern Passion

Golden Crust was born out of a simple, uncompromising obsession: why should authentic Liege pearl waffles and paper-thin Brittany buckwheat galettes only exist in the cobblestone lanes of Brussels and Paris?

In 2018, we restored a retro 1969 Citroën H-Van, shipped over heavy cast-iron clamshell presses from Belgium, and began proofing brioche dough for 24 slow hours. Today, our 742 Sweetbriar sanctuary is a warm living room for breakfast connoisseurs, specialty coffee purists, and sweet-toothed dreamers.

Tienen, Flemish Brabant, Belgium

Tienen Belgian Pearl Sugar (P4 Grade)

Formed by high-pressure compaction of pure sugar beet syrup into dense 4-6mm granules. Crucially, it does not dissolve during our 24-hour dough proofing stage.

Culinary Role in Kitchen

Creates the signature caramelized crunch pockets that pop in the mouth while keeping the interior brioche soft, buttery, and pillowy.

Technical & Thermal Spec

Caramelizes precisely at 185°C (365°F) upon contact with heavy cast iron.

Culinary Chemistry

The Science of the Waffle Grid

Why our pastries taste noticeably different from ordinary brunch diners: precision fermentation, thermal inertia, and radial batter tension.

⚗️

1. 24-Hour Yeast Maturation

Standard diners use chemical baking powder for quick rise. We proof living wild yeast brioche at 4°C for 24 hours. This breaks starches down into complex sweet esters, delivering deep brioche flavor that never feels heavy.

Fermentation Temp: 4°C / 39°F
🔥

2. The 185°C Maillard Window

18kg cast iron thermal mass prevents temperature collapse when cold dough hits the iron. At 185°C, pearl sugar granules melt externally into an amber caramelized crust while staying crystalline inside for dual-texture bliss.

Caramelization Trigger: 185°C / 365°F
🌀

3. 0.8mm Rozell Centrifugal Sweep

Brittany crepe masters use a beechwood T-bar spun in a single radial arc. This spreads buckwheat batter down to micro-thin 0.8mm before steam bubbles create delicate, golden lace edges with tender centers.

Bake Duration: 55 Seconds
